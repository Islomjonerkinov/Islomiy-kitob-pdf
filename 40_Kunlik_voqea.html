Daftarni Yuritish Namunasi

Ushbu sahifa sizga 40 kunlik ma'naviy safaringizni tartibli tashkil etishda yordam beradi

1

1-qadam: Ko'chirib yozish

Har kuni suraning bir qismini yoki to'liq matnini (imkoniyatingizga qarab) maxsus ajratilgan bloknot bo'limiga ko'chiring.

Foydasi: Qo'l harakati orqali qalb tinchlanadi, diqqat jamlanadi va sura so'zlari yadingizda qoladi.
2

2-qadam: Tilovat qilish

Yozib bo'lganingizdan so'ng, o'z qo'lingiz bilan yozgan matningizga qarab, surani ovoz chiqarib, shoshilmasdan o'qing.

Foydasi: O'z yozganingizni o'qish sizga o'zgacha zavq va ishonch bag'ishlaydi.

VOQEA SURASI

بِسْمِ ٱللَّٰهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ

Bismillahir Rohmanir Rohiym

1. Bismillahir Rohmanir Rohiym.

2. Iza vaqa'atil vaqi'ah.

3. Laysa livaq'atiha kazibah.

4. Khofizatur rofi'ah.

5. Iza rujjatil ardu rojja.

6. Va bussatil jibalu bassa.

7. Fakanat haba-am munbassa.

8. Va kuntum azvajan salasah.

9. Fa-ashabul maymanati maa ashabul maymanah.

10. Va ashabul mash-amati maa ashabul mash-amah.

11. Vas-sabiqunas-sabiqun.

12. Ulaaikal muqorrobun.

13. Fiy jannatin-na'iym.

14. Sullatum minal avvaliyn.

15. Va qoliylum minal akhiriyn.

16. 'Ala sururim mavzunah.

17. Muttaki-iyna 'alayha mutaqobiliyn.

18. Yatufu 'alayhim vildanum mukholladun.

19. Biakvaabiv-va abaariyqa va ka'sim mim ma'iyn.

20. Laa yusodda'una 'anha va laa yunzifun.

21. Va faakihatim mimma yatakhoyyarun.

22. Va lakhmi toyrim mimma yashtahun.

23. Va khurun 'iyn.

24. Kaamsalil lu'lu'il maknun.

25. Jazaa-am bimaa kaanu ya'malun.

26. Laa yasma'una fiyha lagvav-va laa ta'siymas.

27. Illaa qiylan salamaan salamaa.

28. Va ashabul yamiyni maa ashabul yamiyn.

29. Fiy sidrim makhzud.

30. Va tolkhim manzud.

31. Va zillim mamdud.

32. Va maa-im maskub.

33. Va faakihatin kasijroh.

34. Laa maqtu'ativ-va laa mamnu'ah.

35. Va furushim marfu'ah.

36. Innaa ansha'naahunna inshaa-a.

37. Faja'alnaahunna abkaaro.

38. 'Uruban atrooba.

39. Li-ashabil yamiyn.

40. Sullatum minal avvaliyn.

41. Va sullatum minal akhiriyn.

42. Va ashabush-shimaali maa ashabush-shimaal.

43. Fiy samumiv-va khamiym.

44. Va zillim miy yahmum.

45. Laa baaridiv-va laa kariym.

46. Innahum kaanu qobla zaalika mutrafiyn.

47. Va kaanu yusirruna 'alal khinsil 'aziym.

48. Va kaanu yaquluna a-iza mitnaa va kunnaa turooban-va 'izoomand a-innaa lamab'usun.

49. A-va aabaa'unal avvalun.

50. Qul innal avvaliyna val akhiriyn.

51. Lamajmu'una ilaa miyqoti yavmim ma'lum.

52. Summa innakum ayyuhad-doollunal mukazzibun.

53. Laa-akiluna min shajarim min zaqqum.

54. Famaali-una minhal butun.

55. Fashaaribuna 'alayhi minal khamiym.

56. Fashaaribuna shurbal hiym.

57. Haaza nuzuluhum yavmad-diyn.

58. Nakhnu khalaqnaakum falav laa tusaddiqun.

59. Afaro-aytum maa tumnun.

60. A-antum takhluqunahu am nakhnul kholiqun.

61. Nakhnu qoddarnaa baynakumul mavta va maa nakhnu bimasbuqiyn.

62. 'Alaa an nubaddila amsaalakum va nunshi-akum fiy maa laa ta'lamun.

63. Va laqod 'alimtumun-nash-atal ulaa falav laa tazakkarun.

64. Afaro-aytum maa takhrusun.

65. A-antum tazro'unahu am nakhnuz-zaari'un.

66. Lav nashaa-u laja'alnaahu khutoman fazoltum tafakkahun.

67. Innaa lamugramun.

68. Bal nakhnu mahrumun.

69. Afaro-aytumul-maa-allaziy tashrabun.

70. A-antum anzaltumuhu minal muzni am nakhnul munzilun.

71. Lav nashaa-u ja'alnaahu ujaajan falav laa tashkurun.

72. Afaro-aytumun-naarallatiy turun.

73. A-antum ansha'tum shajaratahaa am nakhnul munshi-un.

74. Nakhnu ja'alnaahaa tazkirotav-va mataa'al lil muqviyn.

75. Fasabbikh bismi robbikal 'aziym.

76. Falaa uqsimu bimavaaqi'in-nujum.

77. Va innahu laqasamul-lav ta'lamuna 'aziym.

78. Innahu laqur-aanun kariym.

79. Fiy kitaabim maknun.

80. Laa yamassuhu illal mutohharun.

81. Tanziylum mir-robbil 'alaamiyn.

82. Afabihaazal khadiysi antum mudkhinun.

83. Va taj'aluna rizqakum annakum tukazzibun.

84. Falav laa izaa balagatil khulqum.

85. Va antum khiyna-izin tanzurun.

86. Va nakhnu aqrobu ilayhi minkum va laakil laa tubsirun.

87. Falav laa in kuntum goyro madiyniyn.

88. Tarji'unahaa in kuntum sodiqiyn.

89. Fa-ammaa in kaana minal muqorrobiyn.

90. Farovkhuv-va royhaanuv-va jannatu na'iym.

91. Va ammaa in kaana min ashabil yamiyn.

92. Fasalaamul laka min ashabil yamiyn.

93. Va ammaa in kaana minal mukazzibiynaz-zoolliyn.

94. Fanuzulum min khamiym.

95. Va tasliyatu jakhiym.

96. Inna haaza lahuva haqqul yaqiyn. Fasabbikh bismi robbikal 'aziym.
